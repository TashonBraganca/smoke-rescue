Headless benchmark, 25 Oct 2026 (tools/bench; run on RunPod, scored with tools/bench/score_bench.py)

What was run: 30 houses (28 seeded random houses 5001-5028 plus family ranch and long ranch) x 1 seed (seed 0) x 4
variants (full, no_radio, one_robot, baseline) = 120 missions of duration_s 600 with mission_time_limit_s 480, fire,
robot collision and capsule walls on. The brief planned 3 seeds (360 runs); it was cut to seed 0 because the pod's CPU
quota is 27.2 cores (cgroup cfs_quota 2720000 / period 100000), not the 256 it reports, so 360 runs would have taken
7 to 8 hours. All 120 finished, 0 failed. The score file lists the seed 1 and 2 runs as "missing"; they were not run.

Splits: a house counts as "seen" when its seed number appears in config/brain.json or config/perception.json reason
strings or in data/evidence/*.txt (the grep and its matches are printed in the score file). 20 seeded houses are seen;
unseen are 5008, 5009, 5012, 5018, 5019, 5024, 5025, 5026 and both ranches (n = 10). brain.json's end_clear_way_out_m
reason cites runs on both ranches, so calling them unseen is generous.

Statistics: bootstrap 95 % intervals over houses (10,000 resamples) and paired differences full minus each variant
with a Wilcoxon signed-rank p value. With one seed per house the intervals capture house-to-house variation only, not
seed variation.

Caveats:
  * Headless has no rendered camera: YOLO and visual SLAM are not exercised, smoke does not degrade sensing, and person
    confirmation uses the headless sighting model. Results speak to search strategy and coordination, not perception.
  * T90 is a proxy: time until floor swept within 2 m of a robot's path reached 90 % of that house's full-variant
    final ceiling. result.json has no coverage over time. Using full's own ceiling favours full.
  * Fire difficulty: result.json and trace.jsonl hold no air temperature, so peak air temperature and fire head start
    per house could not be reported. The traces do show fire acting on the mission (9 to 11 room_marked_fire events
    per run in the two runs checked). Seeded houses use a fire floor plan snapped to the 0.25 m fire grid (edges move
    at most 0.125 m); walls the robots see are unchanged.
  * The runner was the linux-x64 build on the pod (built 25 Oct 11:44 UTC). Edits to World/FloorPlan.cs, Scenario.cs,
    Atmosphere.cs and RecordingSession.cs made after that build are not in it.
