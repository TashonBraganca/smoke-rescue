        What you are watching. The real robot brain, running headless on AWS. There is no rendered camera: people are detected through the simulator's sighting model (distance, line of sight and smoke). The fire and the robots' true positions come from the simulator and are drawn for you only. The coloured searched floor comes from each robot's own map, which is all the robot knows.

        Real floor plans come from HouseExpo (Li et al. 2019, MIT licence, github.com/TeaganLi/HouseExpo), converted to our floor plan format. People and fire are placed by our scenario generator.
