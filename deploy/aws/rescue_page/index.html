        Place the scene

        Choose a tool, then click or tap the plan. Keep people and fire on clear floor. The server checks door paths before preparing the scene.

        Place up to five people and one fire origin.

        New scenes need a recorded fallback before they can run live. Preparing runs the real brain once, usually 2 to 4 minutes. Your placement is kept while you wait.

        What you are watching. The real robot brain, running headless on AWS. There is no rendered camera. The simulator models sightings using distance, line of sight and smoke. Fire and true robot positions are drawn for the viewer only. Coloured floor comes from each robot's own map. Person markers show the brain's confirmations. The final score compares those reports with hidden person positions and counts each person once.

        Residential dataset plans come from HouseExpo, Li et al., IROS 2020, source. These are synthetic SUNCG-derived layouts converted to our floor plan format. MIT licence and copyright notice.
