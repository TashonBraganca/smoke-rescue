Relocalization report, 25 Oct 2026 (brief B in handoff/brief_next_25oct.txt, from opencv-a2)

Verdict: nothing tested beats our 2D rigid RANSAC on held-out recall, wrong poses and false accepts together, so
place.py and config/perception.json are unchanged. The PnP code is kept as a patch, handoff/reloc_pnp_place.patch.

What was tested, on the fence-fix TUM harness, with the same pairs and split. Tuning is fr2_pioneer_slam + fr1_room;
held-out is fr2_pioneer_360 + fr3_long_office_household. The harness halts unless 'ours' reproduces the committed
numbers (1518/2414 accepted, 70 wrong, 0/6500 false), and that check passed. Thresholds were picked on tuning only.
  * PnP relocalization, the method ORB-SLAM uses: partner 3D points against own 2D keypoints with cv2.solvePnPRansac,
    SOLVEPNP_SQPNP (with RANSAC) and USAC_MAGSAC, projected to the planar link. Own pixel coordinates stay local to
    the robot; the radio payload is unchanged.
  * Matching: mutual nearest neighbour (crossCheck), with and without the ratio test.
  * Temporal consistency: accept pair (i, j) only when (i+1, j+1) agrees within 0.3 m and 4 deg (the brain's
    tolerances); 'either' accepts if the next or the previous pair agrees.
  * Better 32-byte descriptors (BEBLID, TEBLID): not testable. The installed opencv-python 5.0.0 has no contrib, so
    cv2.xfeatures2d is missing. Nothing was installed.

PnP SQPNP cuts held-out wrong poses from 46 to 8, which is the real finding, but it loses 22 held-out accepts, so it
fails the ship rule on recall. An earlier, looser pick, tuned on the sampled tuning negatives only, gave held-out
1267/1601 accepted with 16 wrong and 0 false. It was not used, because on every tuning negative the PnP pick reached
13 inliers against the 10-inlier accept threshold, a false-accept risk the sampled negatives had hidden. That stricter
rule was added after the looser pick's held-out result had been seen, and both results are reported here.

PnP wins on TUM because the wrong poses come from points about 4.9 m away, where Kinect depth noise gives a 0.19 m
median planar residual even under the true link. PnP judges own points by pixel direction, not depth. In our Unity
houses depth is clean, and PnP did worse there: tools/reloc_unity_check.py on two seed-934 runs plus family_ranch gave
pnp_sqpnp 23.7 % recall with 50/711 wrong, against ransac_rigid 26.0 % with 17/780 wrong (both 1/9000 false). That
check used the looser thresholds without the dz gate and was not rerun.

What ground truth enters: only the own camera tilt and height, the same values the 2D pipeline already uses. With one
fixed mount (no per-frame truth at all), PnP still won on pioneer_360: 294 accepted with 14 wrong, against ours at 200
with 51.

Temporal consistency alone removes wrong poses (46 to 17) but costs 12 points of recall on held-out data. Our brain
already requires a second agreeing match, so part of this protection is already in production. The brain's exact
rule, which also needs the two partner keyframes at least 1 m apart, was not tested.
