Fence fix report, 25 Oct 2026 (brief: handoff/brief_fence_fix_25oct.txt, from opencv-a2)

Verdict: the OpenCV fit with uniqueness and spread gates does not beat ours on held-out data, so place.py and
config/perception.json are unchanged.

What was built: tools/fence_fix_compare.py. The new fit (cv2_unique) takes a hypothesis from
cv2.estimateAffinePartial2D(RANSAC), refits it as a strict rigid transform on its inliers with place.fit_rigid, then
applies two gates. Uniqueness: remove the winner's inliers, fit the rest the same way, and reject when runner-up
inliers / winner inliers exceeds a ratio. Spread: the convex hull area (cv2.convexHull + cv2.contourArea) of the
inliers must reach a minimum. I measured spread on the inliers' planar body-frame points, not image points, because
the radio payload carries no image coordinates. Every variant runs on the same match sets with the same accept rule
(10 inliers, RMSE 0.06 m). The harness halts unless 'ours' reproduces the committed 1518/2414, 70 wrong, 0/6500 false
and cv2_ransac reproduces 1594, 126, 3. Both checks passed.

Thresholds were picked on the tuning split only (fr2_pioneer_slam + fr1_room): ratio 0.65, area 0 m^2. The area
gate never helped on tuning, because any area cut that removed wrong poses removed more right ones. Results on the
held-out split (fr2_pioneer_360 + fr3_long_office_household), then pooled:

The ship rule fails on recall. On held-out data, cv2_unique accepts 1191 pairs where ours accepts 1203. It makes 3
fewer wrong-pose accepts (43 vs 46) and has 0 false accepts. Both differences sit inside the noise, so this is a
measured null, not a small win. Pairs share frames, which makes the printed intervals too narrow.

What the gates did on held-out data: the rigid refit alone took cv2_ransac from 82 wrong poses to 55. The uniqueness
gate then took 55 to 43, at a cost of 20 accepts. The spread gate at its tuned value of 0 did nothing. In the Pioneer
hall, which has the fence, wrong poses went from 45 to 42 on pioneer_360 (held-out) and from 20 to 17 on pioneer_slam
(tuning).

Why it does not work: on tuning positives, right and wrong poses look alike to both gates. Runner-up ratio median is
0.10 for right poses and 0.21 for wrong ones, but p90 for right poses is 0.44, so a cut that catches the fence also
drops real revisits. Hull area median is 0.36 m^2 for right and 0.27 m^2 for wrong, with the same overlap. One-to-one
matching with a ratio test already removes most rival panel matches, so the second hypothesis is rarely there to be
found. One signal did separate on tuning data: the inliers of wrong poses lie far away (median range 4.9 m against
1.8 m for right ones). That points at long-range depth on the fence. It is a lead for the next attempt, not
something I tested on held-out data.

Not done because the fit did not win: no place.py change, no place_rigid_fit switch, no new pytest. I did not run
tests/test_place.py or tests/test_place_recognition.py, since no production code changed.
