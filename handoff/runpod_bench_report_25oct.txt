RunPod benchmark report, 25 Oct 2026 (brief: handoff/inbox_helper/01_runpod_bench.txt)

What ran: 30 houses (28 seeded houses plus family ranch and long ranch) x seed 0 x 4 variants = 120 headless missions
of 600 s. All 120 finished, 0 failed. Evidence: data/evidence/bench_25oct_score.{txt,json} and bench_25oct_notes.txt
(commit 9826b7d3).

The brief planned 360 runs (3 seeds). They were cut to 120 because the pod is capped at 27.2 CPU cores even though it
reports 256, so 360 would have taken 7 to 8 hours. Three launch problems were fixed first:
  * /dev/shm is mounted noexec, so run_all.sh now calls itself through sh (commit 575fa611), and dotnet and the
    runner ran from /workspace.
  * A yield watcher stopped the first 120-run attempt at 18:48 IST, when a GPU process appeared for under two
    minutes. The owner then confirmed no one else would use the pod tonight, and the runs restarted without a watcher.
  * The restart wrote into a fresh runs2/ folder, so no stale run markers had to be deleted.

Verdict:
  * Two robots that share context find people about 37 s sooner than two robots with no radio, and 60 s sooner than
    one robot. They also cover more floor, with less double-searching than the no-radio pair.
  * The final share of people found is the same for every variant (94 to 98 %). With 480 s, even one robot finds
    nearly everyone in these houses. The team's advantage is speed, not the final count.
  * The features added since 23 Oct (full vs baseline) buy faster coverage (T90 132 s sooner) and more missions
    ending with both robots out (+0.3). They do not change rooms, people or time to confirm, and the baseline robots
    overlap less.
  * Both out is the cost of the team: full gets both robots out in 70 % of all missions, one_robot in 100 %, and
    no_radio almost never (no shared map of the way out).
  * No false confirmations anywhere, so that metric does not separate the variants headless.

Mission difficulty: result.json and trace.jsonl record no air temperature, so the brief's per-house fire head start
and peak air temperature cannot be reported from these runs. Fire did act on the missions: 9 to 11 rooms per run were
marked as burning and later cleared in the two traces checked. People found sits near 100 % for every variant, so the
missions are probably too easy to separate the variants on that measure. Proposed harder tier:
  * a fire head start before the robots enter, so rooms burn on arrival;
  * mission_time_limit_s 300 instead of 480;
  * people placed in rooms reachable only through another room.

Caveats:
  * Headless has no rendered camera: YOLO and visual SLAM are not exercised, and person confirmation uses the
    headless sighting model.
  * One seed per house, so the intervals capture differences between houses, not between seeds.
  * T90 is a swept-floor proxy measured against full's own ceiling, which favours full.
  * The pod runner was built at 11:44 UTC; later edits to the world C# files are not in it.
  * Calling the ranches unseen is generous: a brain.json reason string cites runs on them.
