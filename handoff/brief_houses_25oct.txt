GOAL
Two more one-floor houses that the same robots search, so judges see the system work on houses it was
not tuned on. The robot brain must not change at all (Assets/SmokeRescue/Brain and config/brain.json
are off limits). Nothing in robot code may know which house it is in.

1. Family ranch: 9 rooms, about 150 to 180 m2. Living, kitchen with dining, hallway, three bedrooms,
   two bathrooms, laundry. Typical US single-storey ranch.
2. Long ranch: 10 rooms, about 180 to 220 m2, one long central hallway with rooms off both sides
   (living, kitchen, family room, four bedrooms, two bathrooms, utility).
   Each house: exactly one entrance (front door), robots launch from pads outside it like seed 934
   ("door" and launch pads as in scenario_house_onedoor_seed934_full.json), 4 or 5 people in different
   rooms using the existing survivor poses, one or two fire origins in config/fire_<house>.json
   following config/fire.json, interior doorways at least 0.9 m wide, smoke rooms like seed 934.
